# 📊 Sales Performance & Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

This interactive Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of sales performance, cost distribution, and product trends across different companies. Designed with a clean, cohesive purple theme, it enables stakeholders to quickly track key performance indicators (KPIs), identify top-performing products, and monitor monthly revenue growth.

## 📈 Dashboard Visuals & Insights

Net Sales By Company (Donut Chart):

Breaks down the $283.80K total net sales across three key companies:

Gel Boomerangs: 49% (Leading revenue contributor)

Colorado Boomerangs: 30%

Tri Fly: 21%

Product By Sales (Column Chart):

Ranks individual products by total sales revenue.

Top Performer: Quad leads significantly with $77K in sales, followed by Yanaki ($41K) and Carlota ($29K).

Lowest Performers: Mt. Fun and Fun Fly sit at $5K each.

Monthly Net Sales Trend (Line Chart):

Tracks revenue fluctuations from January to December.

Peak Performance: December recorded the highest sales at $32.9K, showing a strong year-end surge.

Lowest Dip: September saw the lowest sales at $17.1K, followed by steady recovery in Q4.

Sales and Cost By Company (Clustered Column Chart):

Compares revenue against expenses for each company to evaluate profitability:

Gel Boomerangs: $139.4K Sales vs. $70.9K Cost

Colorado Boomerangs: $85.2K Sales vs. $43.6K Cost

Tri Fly: $59.2K Sales vs. $30.3K Cost
